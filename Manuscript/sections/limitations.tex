\section{Limitations}

This study was performed entirely in silico with data produced from other sources. As such, these results intend to serve as a guide for future studies in terms of what to look at and where to look in terms of \ac{ard} that may place selective pressure on \ac{hiv1} to result in compromises for the benefit of the patient. Furthermore, the statistics performed do not use a complete sample of all possible permutations of epitopes favoring strong \acp{drm} scores. Introducing a filter for the maximum number of \ac{drm} mutations may accommodate this slightly, but future tests would hope to either perform the entire sample set or perform random sampling.
